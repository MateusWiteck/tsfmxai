# Relevance to the thesis

Connects contractive random recurrences with a unique long-run distribution,
clarifying why a stable noisy trajectory can keep fluctuating rather than
converging pointwise to zero.

# Method and main result

A Markov chain is written as an iteration of i.i.d. random Lipschitz maps. Under
integrability conditions and a negative expected log-Lipschitz factor, the chain
has a unique stationary distribution and converges to it geometrically.

# Relation to our approach

The generator's uniform bound below one for every bounded innovation is stronger
than contraction on average. The paper supplies the probabilistic interpretation
of the resulting long-run process; domain invariance is certified separately.

# Limitations

The result assumes regularity and integrability conditions. It does not select
AST operators, compute interval bounds, or enforce a finite invariant domain.
