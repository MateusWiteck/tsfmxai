# Research question and goal

## Research question

How can synthetic discrete-time time-series generators with known generative
mechanisms and explanation ground truths be used to benchmark the ability of
forecasting, system-identification, and explainability methods to recover, use,
and explain the underlying temporal dependencies?

## Research goal

Develop a configurable synthetic benchmark whose known mechanisms provide
structural dependencies, signed coefficients, realized local contributions,
and attribution-based references for every relevant forecast horizon.
