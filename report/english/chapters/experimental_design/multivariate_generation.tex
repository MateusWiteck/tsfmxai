\subsection{Multivariate generation and topology-aware terminal sampling}
\label{sec:multivariate-generation}

The multivariate training generator retains one executable equation per channel:
\begin{equation}
 x_{i,t}=f_i\bigl(\{x_{j,t-\ell}:j\in S_i\}\bigr)+\varepsilon_{i,t}.
\end{equation}
The set $S_i$ specifies permitted sources, rather than mandatory edges. The
implemented topologies include independent channels, a permuted predecessor
chain, and random source subsets. Every channel permits its own past; external
sources are capped by configuration. Each lag occurrence selects a source with
weight three for the target channel and weight one for each external source.
All equations evaluate the same past before the current time step is appended.

The default per-channel mixture assigns zero probability to original ASTs,
50\% to Shao--Wu, one sixth to Jacobian treatment, and one third to Heaps. In this
implementation, Jacobian and Heaps channels use self-only sources: the latter
constructs a scalar AR through the existing Heaps parameterization. Thus these
channels do not implement a coupled VAR block. Jacobian innovations are bounded
uniform draws, whereas the other channels use Gaussian innovations. Individual
certificates do not provide a joint certificate for the mixed system.

Each trajectory starts from a separately sampled chronological history. The
random initializer independently includes a constant, uniform white noise, and
a cosine in each channel, and sums the included components. This startup law
does not imply stationarity. The Jacobian initializer must remain inside its
certified domain. Startup values are excluded from training windows; no extra
burn-in is discarded. Simulation uses float64 and checks both recurrence outputs
and conversion to the output dtype. After an initial attempt and three new
histories, an exhausted trajectory causes the entire configuration-sampled
system to be replaced. Accepted trajectories from that discarded system are
also discarded. Explicitly supplied systems cannot be replaced.

To regulate the original AST prior across curriculum stages, let $q_{ij}$ be
the normalized source-selection weights and define
\begin{equation}
 m_i^{\mathrm{eff}}=\frac{1}{\sum_{j\in S_i}q_{ij}^2},\qquad
 r_i=r_{\min}+(r_{\max}-r_{\min})
 \frac{m_i^{\mathrm{eff}}-1}{m_i^{\mathrm{eff}}-1+\tau}.
\end{equation}
The design increases the terminal lag probability when a channel can consult
its own past and additional series. The purpose is to create more opportunities
for cross-series interactions as the permitted source set grows. Drawing more
lag terminals makes multiple temporal inputs available to product nodes.
Source selection and sampled operators determine whether an actual cross-series
interaction appears. The effective source count accounts for unequal weights
and follows the allowed sources for each channel across curriculum stages.

The probability $r_i$ weights lag terminals during AST construction. Parameter
terminals receive complementary mass and lag labels share their mass equally.
Operator count follows the original combinatorial size prior. Weighted completion
counts condition the resulting tree on the degree limit and mandatory-lag setting.
The defaults are $r_{\min}=0.25$, $r_{\max}=0.75$, and $\tau=1$.
For zero, one and two external sources, these give respectively 25\%, 43.75\%
and 53\% before structural conditioning. This policy applies to original channels.

The corrected comparison uses 500 accepted six-channel systems per topology,
two trajectories of 128 steps, at most four operators, lags 1--16 and degree four.
Context, prediction length and stride are 64, 16 and 16. The explicit diagnostic
mixture includes 40\% originals, 30\% Shao--Wu, 10\% Jacobian and 20\% Heaps
so that the effect of the sampling correction is observable. The seed is 314159.
The experiment record is under
\texttt{experiments/}\allowbreak\texttt{multivariate\_generation/}\allowbreak
\texttt{04\_terminal\_sampling\_correction/}.
