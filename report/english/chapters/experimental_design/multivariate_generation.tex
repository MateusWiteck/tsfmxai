\subsection{Multivariate generation}
\label{sec:multivariate-generation}

The multivariate training generator retains one executable equation per channel:
\begin{equation}
 x_{i,t}=f_i\bigl(\{x_{j,t-\ell}:j\in S_i\}\bigr)+\varepsilon_{i,t}.
\end{equation}
The set $S_i$ specifies permitted sources, rather than mandatory edges. The
implemented topologies include independent channels, a permuted predecessor
chain, and random source subsets. Every channel permits its own past; external
sources are capped by configuration. Each lag occurrence selects a source with
weight three for the target channel and weight one for each external source.
All equations evaluate the same past before the current time step is appended.

The treatment of each channel follows the sampled topology. A channel
with at least one permitted external source uses Shao--Wu and assigns at
least one lag occurrence to an external source; an external KernelSynth
channel counts. A channel without external sources draws Jacobian or Heaps
with equal probability. Under six channels, random topology, and a cap of two
external sources, this yields approximately two thirds Shao--Wu and one sixth
each Jacobian and Heaps before hybrid-family effects. Jacobian and Heaps
channels therefore read only their own past: Heaps constructs a scalar AR
through the Heaps parameterization, not a coupled VAR block. Every sampled
tree contains at least one lag, which Shao--Wu needs to read an external source
and Jacobian needs for its certificate. Heaps samples its lag support
directly, without a tree. Conditional on choosing Heaps,
the support-class probabilities are 40\% consecutive lags $1,\ldots,p$,
20\% one isolated lag $s\in\{2,\ldots,16\}$, and 40\% periodic lags
$s,2s,\ldots,ps$ with $p,s\geq2$ and $ps\leq16$. Pairs are uniform within
each class. These classes contain 16, 15, and 19 disjoint support patterns,
respectively, at the current $L_{\max}=16$.
The stable AR has additive lag terms and no multiplicative lag interactions.
Its reconstructed equation, rather than the raw placeholder, is the valid
explanation target. Jacobian innovations are bounded
uniform draws, whereas the other channels use Gaussian innovations. Individual
certificates do not provide a joint certificate for the mixed system.

Each trajectory starts from a separately sampled chronological history. The
random initializer independently includes a constant, uniform white noise, and
a cosine in each channel, and sums the included components. This startup law
does not imply stationarity. The Jacobian initializer must remain inside its
certified domain. Startup values are excluded from training windows; no extra
burn-in is discarded. Simulation uses float64 and checks both recurrence outputs
and conversion to the output dtype. After an initial attempt and three new
histories, an exhausted trajectory causes the entire configuration-sampled
system to be replaced. Accepted trajectories from that discarded system are
also discarded. Explicitly supplied systems cannot be replaced.
