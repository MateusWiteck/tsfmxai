\section{Implemented Synthetic Time-Series Generator}
\label{sec:implemented-generator}

The implementation generates an executable recurrence before generating its
observations. It retains the expression, numerical parameters, lag assignments,
innovation law, and initialization policy, so several trajectories can be drawn
from the same mechanism. This chapter explains the representation choices,
observed numerical behaviour, and conditions used to construct controlled
generator families. Published mathematical results and the project's
algorithms for satisfying their hypotheses are presented separately.
