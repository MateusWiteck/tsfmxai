\subsubsection{Stationary VAR generation using Heaps' parameterization}
\label{sec:implemented-heaps}

\paragraph{Published result used.}
\citet[Sections~2.1--2.2 and Appendix]{heaps2023stationarity} parameterizes
stationary Gaussian VAR($L$) models through unconstrained matrices and
partial-autocorrelation matrices. Equation~\eqref{eq:heaps-pac-map} maps each
unconstrained singular value $u$ to $u/\sqrt{1+u^2}<1$. The reverse covariance
and coefficient recursions recover the stationary VAR coefficients from
those partial autocorrelations and a positive-definite innovation covariance.
The equivalent companion-matrix condition is $\rho(\mathbf B)<1$.

\paragraph{How the implementation uses it.}
\path{src/tsfmxai/generators/heaps.py} samples the order and unconstrained
matrices, applies the singular-value map, and executes the symmetric-root
reverse recursions. The sampling distributions are project choices: the
recorded comparison uses orders uniformly drawn from 1--12, independent
normal matrix entries with standard deviation 0.45, zero stationary mean,
and innovation covariance $0.05^2 I$. This implements a VAR($L$) family;
VAR(1) is its single-lag case.

The final coefficients are converted to one linear AST per target, including
the intercept required by the selected stationary mean. The constructor
forms the joint lag-state covariance $\mathbf V$ from the reconstructed
autocovariances and checks
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf V=\mathbf B\mathbf V\mathbf B^\top+\mathbf Q,
 \label{eq:implemented-heaps-lyapunov}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf Q$ inserts $\Sigma_\varepsilon$ into the newest lag block.
Independent numerical checks require a companion spectral radius below one,
partial-autocorrelation singular values below one, positive-definite
$\mathbf V$, and a relative Lyapunov residual below $10^{-8}$.

The entire initial lag history is drawn jointly from the corresponding
Gaussian stationary distribution, using a Cholesky factor of $\mathbf V$.
This gives stationary initialization in exact arithmetic, subject to the
reported floating-point checks. It also preserves correlations between
channels and between initial lag positions. Future innovations are sampled
independently. The retained mechanism exposes signed linear coefficients and
variable--lag structure directly; nonlinear products are represented by the
AST families in the preceding subsections.
