\subsubsection{Contraction on an invariant state domain}
\label{sec:implemented-domain-contraction}

The bounded-domain family applies discrete-time contraction to the augmented
lag state. The connection between contraction and incremental stability is
developed by \citet{tran2019convergence}; delay-state augmentation and set
invariance are discussed by \citet{laraba2016setinvariance}. In our scalar
recurrence, $\mathbf{s}_t=(x_t,\ldots,x_{t-L+1})^\top$ and
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal F_\varepsilon(\mathbf{s})=
 \bigl(F_{\mathcal T}(\mathbf{s})+\varepsilon,
       s_0,\ldots,s_{L-2}\bigr)^\top.
 \label{eq:implemented-state-transition}
\end{equation}
For two histories driven by the same innovation, a uniform Jacobian norm
bound below one makes their distance contract. The accompanying condition
$\mathcal F_\varepsilon(D)\subseteq D$ keeps the certificate applicable to
every subsequent state.

\paragraph{How the implementation enforces the conditions.}
\path{src/tsfmxai/generators/contraction.py} propagates interval bounds for
the value and absolute partial derivatives of each AST node on
$D=[-r_D,r_D]^L$. Addition uses sums of bounds; multiplication uses interval
products and the product rule. With weights $w_d=q_w^d$, $q_w>1$, the
weighted infinity norm gives the lag-shift rows gain $q_w^{-1}$. If
$\overline d_d$ bounds the recurrence derivative with respect to $s_d$, a
sufficient bound for $L\geq2$ is
\begin{equation}
 \sup_{\mathbf{s}\in D}\|J_{\mathcal F}(\mathbf{s})\|_{\mathbf w,\infty}
 \leq\max\left\{\sum_{d=0}^{L-1}\overline d_d q_w^d,
                       q_w^{-1}\right\}<1.
 \label{eq:implemented-weighted-contraction}
\end{equation}
For $L=1$, only the recurrence row contributes. Including the shift rows is
essential when several lags are present.

The sampler attaches coefficients to lag leaves and scales the full expression
to satisfy both the derivative budget and the output bound
$\sup_D|F_{\mathcal T}|+b_\varepsilon\leq r_D$. The comparison uses
$r_D=1$, $q_w=1.25$, a target Lipschitz bound of 0.9, and uniform innovations
with $b_\varepsilon=\sqrt{3}\,0.05$, matching the variance of the Gaussian
innovations in the other families. The stored expression includes this scale.
The admitted sums/products have finite derivatives on the box; explicit time
dependence is excluded. The current constructor is univariate. Its certificate
provides pathwise domain containment under the specified bounded innovations,
and its Monte Carlo checks exercise domain containment and derivative bounds.
Uniform initialization inside the box supplies a controlled transient.
