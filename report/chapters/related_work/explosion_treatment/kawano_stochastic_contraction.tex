\subsubsection{Contraction with Stochastic Innovations}

\citet{kawano2024contraction} consider discrete-time stochastic systems of the
form $\mathbf{s}_{t+1}=\mathcal F(\mathbf{s}_t,\xi_t)$. They define uniform
incremental exponential stability in a moment by comparing two trajectories
under the same realization of the stochastic process and bounding the expected
distance between them by a geometrically decreasing quantity. Their conditions
again act on the state Jacobian of the variational system and cover general
stochastic processes, with specializations for independent and identically
distributed and Markov processes.

In the benchmark generator, the innovation is additive and bounded,
$|\varepsilon_t|\le b_{\varepsilon}$. A Jacobian bound that holds for every
allowed state and innovation is stronger than a moment condition and therefore
provides pathwise common-noise contraction: two trajectories receiving the same
innovation sequence forget their different initial histories, while persistent
innovations keep the shared trajectory fluctuating according to its long-run
distribution.
